# Bundled assets

The background music and sound effects were synthesized specifically for this
project and may be used with it. No third-party art, commercial music, or
copyrighted game assets are bundled.

The pronunciation clips in `assets/voice/` are machine-generated with Microsoft
Edge's neural text-to-speech voices (see the README). They are adequate for
personal and classroom use; review them against the terms of the TTS service
before any commercial educational release, and consider replacing them with
recordings by a native speaker — automated Arabic letter-name pronunciation in
particular is worth a human ear.
